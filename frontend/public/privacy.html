SwitchPrep and your data

SwitchPrep is a free interview-preparation site. You can use all of it without an account; your progress is then kept only in your own browser.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets you keep your progress on our server so you can use it on more than one device. When you sign in, we store:

  • your name and email address, as provided by Google, and Google's identifier for your account;
  • what you track on the site: items marked done or for revision, your notes, quiz scores, your daily routine and the start date of your plan.

We never see your Google password, and we do not ask Google for anything beyond your basic profile.

What we do with it

It is used only to show your own progress back to you. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising, and the site has no analytics or tracking.

Deleting it

Open Account & Data and choose Delete my account. This removes your account and everything stored for it from the server straight away. Export progress on the same page gives you a copy first.
